How to use Sennelier Studio

Your data is private. Everything is saved only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

🏠 Home

Your dashboard: progress rings, your stick count, and a quick look at each collection.

🎨 Collection

Tap a colour to mark it owned (✓). Tap the ☆ to add it to your wishlist, and the 🔁 to flag something you need to restock. Use search and the filter to find colours.

🌈 Palette

Your owned colours in rainbow order — a beautiful view of your collection.

🛍️ Shopping

Your wishlist + restock list together, ready to take to the art shop. Tap ✓ Got it when you've bought or restocked something.

📊 Stats

Totals, progress per collection, and your favourite colour families.

📓 Notes & 🖼️ Journal

Write notes, or add photos of your art with a title, date and note.

💾 Backups

Click ⬇ Export to download one file that saves everything. On a new computer or browser, click ⬆ Import and choose that file.

⚠️ Clearing this site's data — or switching device/browser — removes everything. Export a backup now and then.
